01 / PROBLEM STATEMENT

A slower network
isn’t automatically an intrusion.

Bandwidth limits, delay, topology, and workload intensity can all change network observations. IDS datasets need to preserve that context. This project builds a controlled SDN framework that aligns traffic activity, verified operational phases, telemetry, and experiment logs into traceable observation windows.

THE RESEARCH QUESTIONCan we generate and validate SDN observations whose labels remain grounded in what the experiment actually did?

To reproduce the research experiments

  • A Linux-based isolated SDN environment with Mininet, Open vSwitch, Ryu, and OpenFlow 1.3 support.
  • Traffic-generation and measurement tools, traffic-control access, Python, and experiment configuration.
  • Recorded topology and seed values, verified phase events, workload execution intervals, and separate run directories.
  • Aligned flow statistics, RTT samples, UDP measurements, and validation reports before dataset inclusion.

No minimum CPU, RAM, or hardware capacity is claimed here because the supplied evidence does not establish one. The website and packaged datasets can be explored without running the SDN testbed.

05 / OBJECTIVES

What we set out to build.

  1. Generate controlled SDN traffic across different operational conditions and topologies.
  2. Separate workload activity from stable, constrained, recovery, and transition context.
  3. Construct traceable, evidence-grounded observation windows from multiple telemetry sources.
  4. Reduce leakage through run-disjoint partitions and evaluate frozen models prospectively.
  5. Measure whether network behavior changes under constraint and returns toward baseline.

06 / LIMITATIONS & NEXT STEPS

What remains unproven.

  • Independent malicious ground truth remains unverified; 12 reviewer records lacked required adjudication fields.
  • Synthetic probing results do not establish detection of unseen malicious attack families.
  • Intervals within a run are correlated; statistical significance between models was not established.
  • Observed TCP goodput is not independently calibrated link capacity. Zero measured ICMP loss does not establish precise loss fidelity.
  • Broader topologies, independently adjudicated labels, service-impact measurement, and stronger generalization evaluation remain future work.
Read the paper and its limitations ↗

Framework ≠ a finished real-world attack detector. The demonstrated contribution is controlled traffic generation, traceable dataset construction, scoped synthetic-workload evaluation, and network-fidelity measurement.
